MAP Science TEKS planner

Matches each student's fall RIT to Learning Continuum skills, then lists earlier-grade TEKS to review and Biology TEKS to extend. Runs offline; nothing is saved when you close the tab.

Drop the Achievement Status and Growth CSV here
or click to choose the file. Only rows with Subject = Science are used.

How do I do this?
  1. Open MAP Growth Reports
    • Search for or click the NWEA icon in your SSO.
    • Open the MAP Growth drop-down at the top left. The screen changes.
    • Look toward the bottom and click MAP Growth Reports.
  2. Create the Achievement Status and Growth (ASG) CSV
    • Find ASG Projection or Summary. You may need to scroll.
    • Choose your classes: A classes for Fall, B classes for Spring. Fall example: Biology A (012A) and Biology H A (014A). Leave the B classes unchecked.
    • Click Create Spreadsheet. A new window opens in your Reports queue.
    • When the status says Ready to View, click Download to save the CSV.

    Careful: the Quadrant Chart version of the report sits right above the one you want.

  3. Load it here
    • Drop the CSV you downloaded into the box above, or click the box to choose the file.
    • Nothing is saved when you close the tab, so reload the CSV each time you open this tool.
